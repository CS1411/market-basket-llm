# Lesson 2 - How Much Each Item Sells

Sales shares are set level by level (category, product, brand, size), each level normalised on its own, then multiplied. A shop's sales are a long tail.

- Best seller Onion 1 kg is 1.41% of sales; the best seller sells 541 times the slowest.
- Top 10% of SKUs make 47% of sales; 114 SKUs make half, 365 make 80%.
- The 116 sachet/rupee-pack SKUs are 11.6% of the catalogue and 11.8% of sales.
- Changing one brand's share does not move its product's total, because levels are normalised separately.

Takeaway: most items are rare, which is why rare itemsets are hard for any model to see.
