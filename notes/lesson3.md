# Lesson 3 - Background Baskets

100,000 baskets with no planned structure: basket sizes from a negative binomial (mean 8.01 different items, max 40), items drawn without replacement by the Gumbel trick, quantities 1 to 4 with probabilities 0.80/0.14/0.04/0.02.

- Same seed gives the same baskets (checked).
- Most common item (Onion 1 kg) is in 10.8% of baskets.
- Even in random baskets a popular pair (Onion + Potato) co-occurs in 1.31% of baskets by chance. That is the trap: popular items look "associated" with no cause.

Takeaway: chance co-occurrence is real, so the truth has to be measured against what chance alone gives.
